# Intellectual property

doc_id: intellectual-property
updated: 24 October 2026

Who owns work delivered on Qwlee.

## 1. Buyer rights after delivery
<!-- chunk_id: policy:intellectual-property§1 -->

When an order completes and is paid, the buyer receives the commercial rights described in the gig package, unless the parties agreed otherwise in writing on Qwlee.

## 2. Seller portfolio use
<!-- chunk_id: policy:intellectual-property§2 -->

Sellers may show completed work in their portfolio unless the buyer asks them not to for confidential projects.

## 3. Infringement reports
<!-- chunk_id: policy:intellectual-property§3 -->

If you believe content on Qwlee infringes your rights, contact support with the URL and proof of ownership. We may remove content while we review.

## 4. Original work
<!-- chunk_id: policy:intellectual-property§4 -->

Sellers must deliver original work or properly licensed materials. Plagiarism or unlicensed stock can lead to a full refund and account action.
