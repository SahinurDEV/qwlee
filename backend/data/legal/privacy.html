Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Qwlee (“Qwlee”, “we”, “us”) operates the freelance marketplace at this site and related apps. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we process it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It is modelled on common marketplace practices (including those used by platforms such as Fiverr) and is intended to support GDPR and CCPA-style obligations. By creating an account or using Qwlee you acknowledge this policy. Material changes will be posted here with an updated date; where law requires, we will also notify you.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and profile: name, email, username, password hash, role (buyer/seller), bio, skills, location, language, profile and cover images, portfolio, availability, ID-verification documents when you submit them.
  • Orders and payments: gig and order details, prices, escrow status, tips, invoices, payout methods, withdrawal requests, and limited payment-provider references (we do not store full card numbers).
  • Communications: inbox and order chat messages, attachments you upload, support tickets, dispute threads, reviews and ratings.
  • Usage and device: pages viewed, searches, approximate location from IP, browser/device type, cookies and similar identifiers, referral codes, notification preferences.
  • AI-assist context: when you or an admin use AI helpers, short excerpts of dispute/support/search/chat text (after contact masking where applied) may be sent to our AI processor — see section 3.

2. How we use information (purposes and legal bases)

We process personal information to:

  • Provide the marketplace (accounts, gigs, orders, escrow, messaging, reviews) — contract.
  • Take and send payments, clear earnings, fight fraud and keep the marketplace safe — contract and legitimate interests / legal obligations.
  • Customer support and dispute resolution, including optional AI suggestions that a human must approve before money moves — contract and legitimate interests.
  • Improve search, product quality and security (including risk flags and aggregated admin insights) — legitimate interests.
  • Send transactional email and, where you opt in, product news — contract or consent.
  • Comply with law (tax, accounting, law-enforcement requests) — legal obligation.

Where we rely on consent (for example non-essential cookies or marketing), you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share data with processors who help us run Qwlee, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Payment processors (for example Stripe and other methods enabled by the admin) to take payment and pay sellers.
  • Email and infrastructure providers to deliver mail and host the service.
  • OpenRouter and underlying model providers when AI features are enabled: dispute suggestion, support inbox answers, natural-language gig search parsing, fraud second-pass review, and admin insights. We send only the minimum text needed for that feature; contact details are masked where our code does so; AI never refunds, releases escrow, bans users or messages users on its own — a human must act.
  • Law enforcement or regulators when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and the integrity of the marketplace.

OpenRouter’s own privacy terms describe how model providers may process API payloads. Prefer models/settings that disallow training on your content where available.

4. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, remember language/theme, and protect the service. Optional analytics or marketing cookies are used only if you accept them in the cookie banner. See our Cookie Policy for categories and how to change your choice.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and to data portability. On Qwlee you can:

  • Download a copy of much of what we store about you from Profile → Edit → “Download my data”.
  • Delete your account from the same page (anonymised; open orders or pending withdrawals may block deletion until resolved). Orders, payments, messages and reviews needed for the other party’s records may remain labelled “Deleted user”.
  • Update profile details in your account settings.
  • Email privacy@qwlee.com for other requests. We may need to verify your identity.

California / similar US state laws: we do not sell personal information for money. If we “share” data for cross-context behavioural advertising, you may opt out via the cookie banner (reject non-essential cookies) or by contacting privacy@qwlee.com. You will not be discriminated against for exercising privacy rights.

EU/UK users may lodge a complaint with their local supervisory authority.

6. Children

Qwlee is for users aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact privacy@qwlee.com and we will delete it.

7. International transfers

We may process and store information in countries other than your own (including where our hosts and OpenRouter / model providers operate, such as the United States). Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (for example standard contractual clauses or the processor’s equivalent transfer tools).

8. Retention

  • Account and profile data: while your account is active.
  • After account deletion: personal fields are anonymised; financial and order records may be kept up to 7 years for tax, audit, fraud and dispute purposes.
  • Support, dispute and AI suggestion logs: retained as needed to handle the case and improve safety, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Cookies: per the Cookie Policy / browser controls.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, access controls, password hashing, optional 2FA). No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: privacy@qwlee.com. General support: Support or info@qwlee.com.
