# Payment terms

doc_id: payment-terms
updated: 24 October 2026

How money moves on Qwlee: what buyers pay, what sellers earn, and when earnings can be withdrawn.

## 1. What buyers pay
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§1 -->

Buyers pay the price of the gig package and any extras, plus a service fee. Orders under a threshold also carry a small order fee. Tips carry the percentage service fee. All fees are shown before you pay.

## 2. Payment protection
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§2 -->

Your payment is held by Qwlee, not the seller, until the order completes. If an order is cancelled, see Refunds & cancellations for what is returned.

## 3. What sellers earn
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§3 -->

Sellers receive the order price minus Qwlee's commission, which is shown in the seller's earnings for each order. The commission also applies to tips and to extras added during an order.

## 4. Clearance period
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§4 -->

Earnings become available to withdraw after a clearance period. Until then they show as pending clearance. This gives time to deal with refunds and chargebacks.

## 5. Withdrawals
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§5 -->

Minimums apply per payout method. After you add or change payout details, you can withdraw to them after a hold period. Your payout provider may charge its own fees or currency conversion.

## 6. Chargebacks and negative balances
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§6 -->

If a buyer's payment is reversed after the seller was paid, the amount is deducted from the seller's pending or available balance, which may become negative until new earnings cover it. Filing a chargeback for an order you received can lead to account restrictions.

## 7. Taxes
<!-- chunk_id: policy:payment-terms§7 -->

You are responsible for any taxes on what you buy or earn. Qwlee may collect tax details where the law requires it.
