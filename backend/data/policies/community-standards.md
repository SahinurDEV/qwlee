# Community standards

doc_id: community-standards
updated: 24 October 2026

Rules for using Qwlee fairly and safely.

## 1. Be honest about who you are
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§1 -->

Use one personal account, with your real name and accurate details. Sellers may be asked to verify their identity. Do not create accounts for someone else, share your login, or buy, sell or rent accounts.

## 2. Keep work and payments on Qwlee
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§2 -->

Do not take payments or deliverables off Qwlee to avoid fees. Off-platform contact details in messages may be hidden or flagged.

## 3. Communicate with respect
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§3 -->

No harassment, threats, hate speech, discrimination or sexual content in messages, gigs, reviews or profiles. Don't send bulk unsolicited messages.

## 4. Offer real services, described accurately
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§4 -->

Gigs must describe a service you will personally deliver, at the price and within the time shown. Don't promise results you can't control.

## 5. Services we don't allow
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§5 -->

Illegal services, academic cheating, spam, malware, weapons, adult sexual services, and impersonating a person, brand or government body are not allowed.

## 6. Reviews must be genuine
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§6 -->

Only buyers who completed an order can leave a public review. Offering money or extras in exchange for a good review is not allowed.

## 7. How we enforce these standards
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§7 -->

We may warn, restrict, suspend or permanently ban accounts that break these rules. Serious cases may be reported to authorities.

## 8. Age
<!-- chunk_id: policy:community-standards§8 -->

You must be at least 18 to use Qwlee.
