# Terms of service (dispute-relevant excerpts)

doc_id: terms-excerpt
updated: 24 October 2026

Excerpts used by the dispute assistant. Full terms remain on the site.

## 1. Escrow
<!-- chunk_id: policy:terms-excerpt§1 -->

Buyer payments are held by Qwlee until an order completes, is cancelled, or a dispute is resolved.

## 2. Disputes
<!-- chunk_id: policy:terms-excerpt§2 -->

Disputes should first be raised with the other party. Unresolved disputes can be escalated to Qwlee's trust & safety team for mediation. Admin resolutions are binding on the order.

## 3. Evidence
<!-- chunk_id: policy:terms-excerpt§3 -->

Communication and files kept on Qwlee are the primary evidence for dispute decisions. Off-platform claims are harder to verify.

## 4. Admin authority
<!-- chunk_id: policy:terms-excerpt§4 -->

Qwlee may refund the buyer in full or in part, release funds to the seller, or cancel an order when mediating a dispute.
